Discover Hunza

About
Hunza

A Quiet Longevity Resort in Turkey

Hunza Longevity is located in Kartepe, Turkey, in a calm and naturally rich environment surrounded by forests and open green landscapes, offering a peaceful setting for rest and disconnection.

The property is in close proximity to Lake Sapanca, with easy access to nature and open surroundings. The wider area is known for its forests and varied terrain, offering opportunities for outdoor activities such as walking, nature exploration, and seasonal sports, alongside nearby destinations such as Ormanya, Maşukiye, Pembe Kayalar, and Acarlar Longozu.

Hunza Longevity combines this natural setting with a structured wellness experience, creating a place where comfort and simplicity support both relaxation and an active connection to the outdoors.

Hunza Turks

The name Hunza is inspired by the Hunza Turks, a community long associated with a natural, balanced way of living, where health, vitality, and harmony are part of everyday life. This philosophy deeply influenced the vision of Hunza, where nature's offerings are combined with knowledge, experience, and a holistic understanding of well-being.

Why Hunza
Precision Over Generic

No two programs at Hunza are identical. Your profile, goals, existing conditions, and personal history determine every element of your experience, from the first assessment to the final consultation.

Medical & Holistic, Together

We believe that lasting well-being requires both rigorous medical insight and genuine human care. Our team brings clinical expertise alongside warmth, creating an environment where transformation is not forced but cultivated.

Results That Last

The program concludes with a post-stay medical follow-up consultation to ensure that gains are sustained. We consider your time with us the beginning of a longer journey, not a standalone event.

A Space Without Compromise

From sleep-optimized rooms and private verandas to curated nutrition and specialist-led movement, every element of the environment at Hunza has been designed to remove friction from healing.
