A Region Worth Exploring

Stay deeply, then wander
into the green.

Hunza was built into a landscape that asks to be walked, breathed, and remembered. Around us lie forests stitched with old paths, a glassy lake reflecting the changing sky, rose-toned rock formations sculpted slowly over centuries, and one of Türkiye's last great floodplain forests — each a quiet companion to your stay.

These are not detours from the experience. They are part of it. We invite you to step away for an afternoon, return at twilight, and let the rhythm of the region settle into your stay alongside the rituals of the estate.

Six Places Worth the Walk

A quiet portrait of
the land around us.

Each of these places holds a different kind of stillness. Some ask for a long, slow afternoon; others reward an early start and a thermos of tea. We've gathered our favourites here so you might choose, in your own time.

01 · Ormanya
Forest & Wildlife Kartepe

Ormanya

A 2,000-acre living forest where the mountain breathes most clearly.

Set into the green heart of Kartepe, Ormanya is a vast natural life park threaded with walking and cycling paths, picnic clearings, and quiet observation areas. Birdsong, cool forest air, and the soft hush of leaves make it an ideal place to step out of pace and into presence — a short drive from Hunza, but a world apart.

Lake & Wellness Sakarya

Sapanca Lake

A still mirror of water, ringed by paths made for slow days.

Sapanca's quiet lake is the kind of place that rewards an unhurried morning. Lined with walking and cycling trails, framed by green hills, and softened by the call of waterbirds, it offers a gentle counterpoint to the deeper stillness of the forest. Lakeside cafés, small piers, and family-run trout houses provide quiet pauses along the way.

03 · Pembe Kayalar
Geological Wonder Sakarya

The Pink Rocks

Sculpted hues that glow softly at the edges of the day.

Known locally as Pembe Kayalar, these rose-hued rock formations are a quiet revelation: an unusual stretch of sculpted stone whose colour deepens at sunrise and sunset. A favourite of photographers and slow wanderers, the site rewards those who arrive early or linger into the golden hour, when the rocks take on their most striking blush.

Mountain & Adventure Kocaeli

Kartepe

A mountain for every season — from quiet treks to powdered slopes.

Kartepe is the mountain that shapes Hunza's air and views. In winter its slopes welcome skiers and snowshoers; in warmer months its trails open out into wide trekking routes and panoramic viewpoints. From its higher reaches, the lakes and forests of the region unfold below in a single, quiet sweep.

05 · Acarlar Longozu
Wetland & Wildlife Sakarya

Acarlar Longozu

Where forest and water meet, and time appears to pause.

One of Türkiye's last great floodplain forests, Acarlar Longozu is a rare and protected ecosystem where ancient trees rise straight from still, dark water. Birdsong, soft reflections, and the quiet rhythm of canoes mark the experience here. It is a place for slow attention — for those who wish to listen rather than do.

Village & Waterfalls At Our Doorstep

Maşukiye

Our home village — a place of waterfalls, fresh trout, and gentle conversation.

Maşukiye is the village in which Hunza sits — a green pocket of waterfalls, mountain streams, and family-run trout farms. Its lanes are easily walked, its restaurants warm and unfussy, and its atmosphere quietly welcoming. For many guests, a slow afternoon here is a natural extension of the estate itself.

A Region Through the Seasons

Each season, a different
quiet to discover.

The land around us is never the same place twice. Where you walk in spring is not where you walk in autumn — and the rhythm of your stay can be shaped accordingly.

Spring
March — May
  • Acarlar Longozu in full bloom
  • Wildflower trails through Ormanya
  • Soft mornings on the lake
  • Mild walking weather throughout
Summer
June — August
  • Cooler microclimate than the coast
  • Sapanca boat outings & swims
  • Long, slow forest walks
  • Maşukiye's waterfalls at full flow
Autumn
September — November
  • Forests turning gold & rust
  • Best photography at the Pink Rocks
  • Quiet lake walks at dusk
  • Seasonal harvests on the table
Winter
December — February
  • Snow on the slopes of Kartepe
  • Skiing & snowshoeing nearby
  • Crisp, clear panoramic mornings
  • Warm village lunches by the fire
