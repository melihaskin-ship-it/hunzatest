What is Hunza

A boutique longevity retreat
in the heart of Kartepe.

Hunza Longevity is a boutique wellness retreat created for those who want to step away from the noise of daily life and return to a more balanced rhythm.

Set within the green landscape of Kartepe, Kocaeli, Hunza brings together nature, nutrition, movement, rest, and mindful renewal in an intimate setting designed around personal wellbeing. More than a place to stay, Hunza is a space to reset — physically, mentally, and emotionally.

Here, longevity is not treated as a trend. It is approached as a way of living better: with more awareness, more balance, and more connection to the body's natural needs.

A way of living shaped by nature, simplicity, and time
The Origin of the Name

Inspired by
the Hunza Turks.

The name Hunza is drawn from the Hunza Turks, a community whose long lifespans, vitality into older age, and balanced way of life have inspired researchers and wellness practitioners for generations.

Their daily life is built on simple, enduring principles — natural seasonal nutrition, regular physical activity in the open air, mindful rest, fasting practices, and a close connection to the rhythms of nature. These are not quick fixes, but habits cultivated over a lifetime.

It is from this enduring philosophy that Hunza Longevity draws its name and its purpose — to translate the timeless principles of long, conscious living into a refined, modern setting in the heart of Kartepe.

The Place

A natural pause

Set within the green folds of the Kartepe Mountains in Kocaeli, Hunza occupies a rare pocket of stillness — a place where the air is clean, the forests are dense, and the lakes hold the light of every season.

Equally distant from Istanbul, Bursa, and Ankara, the location is at once retreat and gateway: deep enough into nature to truly disconnect, yet close enough to reach in just over an hour. Step through the door and you arrive in another rhythm entirely.

The Founder

Nine years of learning,
one clear philosophy.

I was born in İzmir in 1974 and spent nearly eighteen years working in communications and journalism. For many years, my life moved at the rhythm of deadlines, cities and constant change. When I settled in Kartepe with my family, life closer to nature slowly began to change how I looked at health, balance and time.

Hunza did not begin as a large retreat. It began in a small apartment, as a women-focused healthy living space built around movement, nutrition and personal guidance. At first, it was simple: pilates, dietitian support and carefully designed programs. But over time, I realized that people were not only looking for exercise or diet plans. They were looking for a more complete way to feel well.

That realization became the beginning of a nine-year journey. Together with dietitian and long-time collaborator Türkan Dayıoğlu, I studied, trained, travelled and experienced different wellness and longevity practices. We visited centers, stayed in camps, observed many approaches and asked one question again and again: what truly helps a person feel renewed — physically, mentally and emotionally?

Hunza Longevity is the result of that long preparation.

Opened in Kartepe in October 2025, Hunza is a boutique retreat surrounded by nature, inspired by the long-life philosophy associated with the Hunza people. But for me, longevity is not only about adding years to life. It is about adding awareness, balance and quality to the years we already have.

"

My aim is not simply to offer a longer life. It is to offer a more conscious one — a way of living with awareness, discipline and respect for the body's natural rhythm.

Tuğba Ciğerdelen Founder · Hunza Longevity
The Methodology

Six pillars,
one complete approach.

Every Hunza program is built on the same foundational architecture — six interconnected pillars, each tailored to the individual, that together form a complete framework for long, healthy living.

Medivo
Health & Healing

Medical insight integrated with holistic care — assessments, expert consultations, and personalized guidance from physicians, dietitians, and physiotherapists.

Calmio
Mind & Relaxation

Mental balance and deep relaxation through meditation, breathwork, hydrotherapy, and heat-based rituals such as sauna and hammam.

Nutrivo
Food & Nutrition

Personalized meal plans built around fresh, organic, seasonal ingredients — nourishment as ritual, designed to support energy and longevity.

Movio
Body & Movement

Pilates, yoga, functional training, swimming, and outdoor practice — movement adapted to your body, guided by personal trainers throughout your stay.

Bello
Beauty & Anti-Aging

Facial treatments, body care, manicure, and personal grooming — looking as good as you feel, with attention to skin health and natural regeneration.

Dormio
Sleep & Rest

Sleep-optimized rooms, premium bedding, and protocols designed for the conditions of true restorative sleep — the foundation of every other pillar.

What We Stand For

Quietly committed
to natural living.

Hunza is built on principles that go beyond the experience itself. The way we source, the way we build, and the way we work with our region all reflect a single belief — that a longer, healthier life depends on a healthier planet to live it on.

i.
Organic & Local
Ingredients drawn from our own kitchen garden and from trusted local producers, free of chemical fertilizers and harmful pesticides.
ii.
Reduced Plastic Footprint
A conscious commitment across the property to minimize single-use plastic and choose reusable, natural alternatives wherever possible.
iii.
Architecture in Harmony
Twelve private bungalows built with natural, regional materials, designed so that comfort and the surrounding landscape are never in conflict.
iv.
Rooted in Kartepe
Our team is recruited and trained from the Kartepe and İzmit area, and we partner with local artisans, producers, and the regional municipality.
