The Invitation

A few days to stop,
and return to yourself.

The Hunza Transformation Retreat is a special experience that brings together Hunza Longevity’s healthy-living philosophy, Serdal Demirci’s subconscious transformation work, Functional Medicine Dietitian Türkan Dayıoğlu’s nutrition approach, and Sports Trainer Nazan Horoz’s movement-focused practice — all within Hunza’s peaceful, nature-immersed estate.

Over these four days you won’t simply rest. You’ll learn to listen to yourself again, support your body, and gain sustainable habits you can carry into everyday life. Real transformation sometimes begins with something simple: a few quiet days that give you permission to come home to yourself.

Your Facilitators

Guided by specialists

A multidisciplinary team, each leading their own field, brought together for these four days.

Serdal Demirci
Subconscious Transformation Expert & Breath Therapist

Leads the retreat’s mental, emotional and spiritual arc: subconscious reprogramming, transformative breath sessions, Reiki, ThetaHealing and EFT, ancestral-clearing meditations, awareness circles, and the opening and closing rituals — holding the group’s process safely throughout.

Türkan Dayıoğlu
Functional Medicine Dietitian

Performs each guest’s detailed body analysis with a one-to-one review, plans the Longevity nutrition programme for the retreat, and provides personal nutrition counselling — including guidance you can sustain long after the retreat ends.

Nazan Horoz
Sports Trainer & Movement Specialist

Designs and leads the physical flow: morning energy activation, Pilates and Aqua Gym sessions, functional movement and posture work, mobility and spine health, and end-of-day stretching — with individual guidance so every guest moves safely.

Samin Alizadeh
Sound Meditation Instructor

Guides the Floating Sound Healing experience on water and evening sound meditations — deep relaxation journeys with Tibetan bowls and therapeutic instruments that settle the nervous system and quiet the mind.

Bade Uça
Yoga & Meditation Instructor

Holds the daily yoga rhythm: grounding and welcome yoga, morning flows to open the day, deep-relaxation Yoga Nidra in the evenings, guided meditation and body-scan work, and the closing yoga and intention meditation.

Ahmet Şanlı
Executive Chef · Longevity Cuisine

Creates the retreat’s Longevity dining experience in coordination with the dietitian — seasonal, refined-sugar-free menus, functional snacks and detox drinks, prepared with techniques that preserve nutritional value, and adapted for allergies and special diets.

Longevity Nutrition

Nourished from
the inside out

Throughout the retreat, the Hunza kitchen prepares every meal with care — designed to support your body from within: wholesome breakfasts, Longevity-inspired meals, gluten- and sugar-free snacks, and herbal teas and detox drinks throughout the day.

Your Stay

Rest in comfort,
surrounded by nature

Set on a lush ten-acre estate in the unspoiled nature of Kartepe, Kocaeli, Hunza offers twelve private VIP villas where comfort and privacy come first. Accommodation is arranged on a shared-room basis (up to two guests per room); shared-room requests can be made at reservation and are accommodated subject to availability.

  • 60 m² room with King bed & private veranda
  • Climate control & underfloor heating
  • Bathtub & shower with organic bath amenities
  • Kettle, herbal teas, work desk & large seating area
  • Spa: salt room, steam room, sauna, Turkish hammam, ice bath & rest lounge
  • Indoor pool, functional training, cardio, yoga & pilates rooms
  • Body analysis with a dietitian · vegetarian full board · free Wi-Fi & parking
