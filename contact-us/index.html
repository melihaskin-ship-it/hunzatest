Before You Reach Out

A few common questions.

How quickly will someone get back to me?
+

Every message is read and answered personally by a member of our team, typically within a few hours and always within 24. For more time-sensitive questions, WhatsApp tends to be the quickest channel.

Do I need to book a consultation before arriving?
+

Not at all — though many guests find it helpful. A complimentary consultation lets us understand your goals and recommend the right program before your stay begins. It is offered by phone or video, at no cost and with no obligation.

Can you arrange a private transfer from the airport?
+

Yes. Private transfers from Istanbul Sabiha Gökçen and Istanbul airports can be arranged on request — and are complimentary for guests booking one of our signature programs. Simply mention this at the time of booking and we will take care of the rest.

I'd like to host a small group retreat. Is that possible?
+

We welcome small private groups and corporate retreats throughout the year. Please write to us at info@hunza.com.tr with your dates and approximate group size, and we will tailor a proposal for you.

Is my information kept private?
+

Always. Anything you share with us — from health questions to travel preferences — remains strictly confidential within our specialist team. We do not share personal data with third parties under any circumstance.
