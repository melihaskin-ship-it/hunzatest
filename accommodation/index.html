About the Accommodation

Designed for deep rest
— nothing more.

At Hunza, accommodation is not an afterthought to the wellness experience — it is part of it. Every villa has been designed around a single question: what does the body actually need in order to rest, repair, and return to balance?

The answers shaped every detail. Acoustic isolation so the night is genuinely silent. Underfloor heating and air conditioning calibrated for the seasons. Hypoallergenic bedding, natural wool pillows, and linens that breathe. High ceilings, generous glass, and a private veranda that opens onto the gardens.

The result is not a hotel room. It is a considered environment for healing — quiet, generous, and yours alone for the duration of your stay.

Our Accommodation

Every villa at Hunza is built to the same considered standard. There are no upgrades, no categories, no tiered experiences — only twelve generous, identical spaces, each offered in turn.

A free-standing pavilion among the olive trees, with a lofted ceiling, large glazed openings, and a private veranda. Inside, a premium king-size bed dressed in natural wool, a generous bathroom with both bathtub and shower, a personal dressing and grooming area, a discreet work space, and a spacious seating area for slow afternoons.

Our Philosophy

Why
Hunza

Designed Around Rest
Every villa at Hunza was designed by asking what the body actually needs to recover — not what looks impressive in a photograph. The result is a space that does its work quietly.
A Setting That Heals
Olive groves, gardens, the lake and the distant mountains. The wider estate is part of the experience — designed so that even the walk back to your villa becomes a moment of restoration.
Materials That Matter
Solid wood, natural stone, linen, wool. Nothing synthetic, nothing precious. Materials that age gracefully, breathe with the body, and quiet the senses.
Service Without Performance
Our team is attentive in the way that genuinely helps — present when you need them, invisible when you don't. The intention is care, not display.
"We are not building a hotel. We are building a quiet place where people remember how to be still."

Hunza was conceived as an integrated retreat where every element — architecture, nutrition, therapy, and rest — serves a single intention: a return to balance. Accommodation is not separate from this; it is the foundation.

The result is a guest experience that feels quieter, slower, and more considered than what is usually offered — because what is usually offered is not enough for what people actually need.
