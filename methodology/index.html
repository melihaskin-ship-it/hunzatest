Questions & Answers

Common
questions

We understand that choosing the right wellness path deserves clarity. If your question is not answered below, our team is available to respond personally.

Ask a Specialist
What is the Hunza Method?+
The Hunza Method is a personalised, integrated approach to long-term well-being. It brings together medical insight, personalised nutrition, mindful movement, restorative therapies, beauty rituals and sleep optimisation across six interconnected pillars — Medivo, Calmio, Movio, Nutrivo, Bello and Dormio — guided by a multidisciplinary team.
Is every program fully personalised?+
Yes. Every program at Hunza is shaped through a combination of professional assessment, expert consultation and personalised care. From the initial health check to the post-stay follow-up, every element is connected to your goals, your condition and your lifestyle. No two journeys are identical.
Why is a medical consultation required?+
For selected programs, a comprehensive medical consultation allows our physician to understand your current health status, medical history and physical condition. This ensures every treatment and activity is selected responsibly, adapted to your capabilities, and protects your body from unnecessary strain. Pre-existing medical conditions should be stabilised before arrival.
What role does the dietary consultation play?+
Nutrition is treated as an essential part of the healing process, not a separate service. Each guest receives personalised dietary guidance from a qualified dietitian. Through an assessment of your eating habits, body composition, goals and preferences, we create a tailored plan that supports energy, recovery and balance — therapeutic, yet always enjoyable.
How do the six pillars work together?+
Each pillar represents a vital dimension of well-being — health, mind, movement, nutrition, beauty and rest. They are not separate experiences but work in synergy. The medical foundation of Medivo informs nutrition in Nutrivo; the calm cultivated in Calmio supports the rest of Dormio; the movement of Movio is shaped around what your body can sustain. Together they form one complete framework.
What happens after my stay ends?+
The Hunza journey does not end when your stay is complete. Guests may take part in a post-stay medical follow-up consultation, available online or by phone, where our physician reviews your progress and provides recommendations to help you maintain the benefits achieved during your stay — supporting a smooth transition back into daily life.
Is the method suitable for those with existing conditions?+
Guests with well-managed existing conditions are warmly welcomed. All activities and therapies are thoughtfully tailored to your individual capabilities by our medical and wellness team. Pre-existing conditions should be stabilised before arrival, and we kindly ask that you share anything relevant in advance so we can prepare your program with the appropriate care.
