02What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device and to remember information about your visit, such as your language preference or how you arrived on the page.

Cookies can be distinguished in several ways:

  • By origin: first-party cookies are set by Hunza; third-party cookies are set by external providers (for example, Google or Meta).
  • By duration: session cookies are deleted when you close your browser; persistent cookies remain on your device until they expire or are deleted.
  • By purpose: see the categories described below.

In addition to cookies, our website may use similar technologies such as pixels, web beacons, and local storage. For simplicity, this Policy refers to all of them as "cookies".
