02Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data in three main ways:

a) Data you provide directly to us

  • Identification & contact data: first and last name, email address, phone number, country of residence, and details submitted through consultation, booking, or contact forms.
  • Booking & stay data: arrival and departure dates, programme of interest, accommodation preferences, dietary requirements, and special requests.
  • Health & wellness data: where you choose to share it, this may include health history, lifestyle information, and goals. Such data is treated as a special category and processed only with your explicit consent.
  • Communications: the content of emails, WhatsApp messages, phone calls, and other correspondence with our team.

b) Data we collect automatically

  • Device & usage data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, time spent on the site, and clicks.
  • Cookies & similar technologies: as described in our Cookie Policy.

c) Data we receive from third parties

  • Aggregated analytics data from advertising and measurement partners such as Google and Meta.
  • Booking and referral information from travel partners or agencies, where applicable.

03How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries and consultation requests.
  • To prepare, confirm, and manage bookings and on-site experiences.
  • To personalise proposed programmes, including health-related recommendations.
  • To send operational communications such as booking confirmations, programme details, and follow-ups.
  • To send marketing communications about programmes, retreats, and special offers where you have consented.
  • To measure and improve the performance of our website, content, and advertising.
  • To prevent fraud, ensure security, and comply with legal obligations.

04Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the purpose, we rely on one of the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide services you have booked.
  • Consent — for marketing communications, non-essential cookies, and processing health-related data.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, and improve our website and services, provided your rights do not override these interests.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with tax, accounting, and regulatory requirements applicable in Türkiye.

06Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf, including hosting, email, CRM, payment, analytics, and marketing providers.
  • Medical and wellness professionals involved in delivering your programme, strictly to the extent necessary.
  • Public authorities where required by law, regulation, or a valid legal request.
  • Successors in a merger, acquisition, or reorganisation, subject to equivalent confidentiality safeguards.

07International Transfers

Some service providers are located outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area, notably in the United States. Where this is the case, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms recognised under Turkish and EU data protection law.

08Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Enquiry data: up to 24 months from your last contact.
  • Booking and stay records: up to 10 years, to comply with Turkish accounting and tax obligations.
  • Marketing data: until you unsubscribe or otherwise withdraw consent.
  • Cookies: as specified in our Cookie Policy.

09Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include encryption in transit, restricted access controls, and regular review of processing activities. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

10Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Request transfer of your data to another controller.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, including Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

To exercise these rights, contact info@hunza.com.tr. We will respond within the legally prescribed timeframes.

11Children

Our services and website are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided data without parental consent, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates the most recent version. Material changes will be communicated by appropriate means.
