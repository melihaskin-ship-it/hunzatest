02Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data in three main ways:

a) Data you provide directly to us

  • Identification & contact data: first and last name, email address, phone number, country of residence, and any additional details you submit through our consultation, booking, or contact forms.
  • Booking & stay data: arrival and departure dates, programme of interest, accommodation preferences, dietary requirements, and any special requests.
  • Health & wellness data: where you choose to share it (for example, when requesting a medical or longevity programme), this may include health history, lifestyle information, and goals. Such data is treated as a special category and processed only with your explicit consent.
  • Communications: the content of emails, WhatsApp messages, phone calls, and any other correspondence with our team.

b) Data we collect automatically

  • Device & usage data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, time spent on the site, and clicks.
  • Cookies & similar technologies: as described in our Cookie Policy.

c) Data we receive from third parties

  • Aggregated analytics data from advertising and measurement partners (e.g., Google, Meta).
  • Booking and referral information from travel partners or agencies, where applicable.

03How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and consultation requests.
  • To prepare, confirm, and manage your booking and on-site experience.
  • To personalise the programme proposed to you, including health-related recommendations.
  • To send you operational communications (booking confirmations, programme details, follow-ups).
  • To send you marketing communications about our programmes, retreats, and special offers, where you have consented.
  • To measure and improve the performance of our website, content, and advertising.
  • To prevent fraud, ensure security, and comply with our legal obligations.

04Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the purpose, we rely on one of the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the services you have booked.
  • Consent — for marketing communications, non-essential cookies, and the processing of any health-related data.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, and improve our website and services, provided your rights do not override these interests.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with tax, accounting, and other regulatory requirements applicable in Türkiye.

06Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf (hosting, email, CRM, payment processing, the analytics and marketing partners listed above).
  • Medical and wellness professionals involved in delivering your programme, strictly to the extent necessary.
  • Public authorities where required by law, regulation, or a valid legal request.
  • Successors in the context of a merger, acquisition, or reorganisation, subject to equivalent confidentiality safeguards.

07International Transfers

Some of our service providers are located outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area (notably in the United States). Where this is the case, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms recognised under Turkish and EU data protection law.

08Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Enquiry data: up to 24 months from your last contact.
  • Booking and stay records: up to 10 years, to comply with Turkish accounting and tax obligations.
  • Marketing data: until you unsubscribe or otherwise withdraw your consent.
  • Cookies: as specified in our Cookie Policy.

09Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encryption in transit, restricted access controls, and regular review of our processing activities. No method of transmission over the internet is, however, completely secure.

10Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request the deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Request the transfer of your data to another controller (data portability).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority — in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu, KVKK).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@hunza.com.tr. We will respond within the legally prescribed timeframes.

11Children

Our services and website are not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data without parental consent, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete such data.

12Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always indicate the most recent version. Material changes will be communicated to you by appropriate means.
