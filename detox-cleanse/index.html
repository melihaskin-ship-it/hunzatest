About the Program

A structured reset for lightness, energy, and internal balance.

The Detox & Cleanse Program supports the body's natural renewal processes while encouraging balance and overall well-being. A thoughtful combination of nutrition, gentle movement, restorative therapies, and lifestyle practices promotes physical lightness, sustained energy, and a greater sense of internal balance.

A carefully composed structure encourages awareness, daily rhythm, and long-term sustainability. It is well suited for individuals looking to reset routines, reconnect with healthier habits, and experience a more balanced and energized state.

The program is fully customized for each participant and continuously adapted to individual capabilities throughout the stay.

Questions & Answers

Common
questions

We understand that choosing the right cleanse deserves clarity. If your question is not answered below, our team is available to respond personally.

Ask a Specialist
Who is this program best suited for?+
The Detox & Cleanse Program is designed for individuals 21 and above who are in generally stable health and wish to support their body's natural renewal processes. It suits those looking to reset routines, reconnect with healthier habits, and experience greater energy and lightness — without the depletion of aggressive cleanses.
Is the program fully personalized?+
Yes, entirely. The program is customized before your arrival based on your current state and goals, and is continuously adapted throughout your stay by our specialist team. No two cleanses at Hunza are the same.
What happens after I book?+
Following your booking, a member of our specialist team will contact you to begin your pre-arrival assessment and program planning. We prepare your personalized schedule, meal plan, and treatment selection before you arrive so that your first day is fully productive.
How do I receive the program brochure?+
Upon submitting your email in the brochure section above, you will receive the full Program Guide directly to your inbox, typically within a few minutes. It covers the complete program structure, session schedules, pillar breakdowns, and next steps.
Will I be hungry during the cleanse?+
No. The Nutrivo detox meal plan is built around fresh, nourishing ingredients designed to satisfy while supporting renewal. The program is not about deprivation — it is about giving the body what it needs to function at its best, with natural therapeutic drinks and mindful eating sessions woven throughout.
Which program duration would you recommend?+
A minimum of 3 days is advised for a meaningful reset. The 5-day program allows for deeper physiological adaptation, while the 7-day program is suited to those seeking a complete cleanse and lasting habit change. Our free consultation will help determine the ideal duration for your goals.
Is follow-up support included after I leave?+
Yes. All programs include an online expert follow-up consultation to help you embed the new habits into daily life and ensure that your progress is sustained once you return home.
